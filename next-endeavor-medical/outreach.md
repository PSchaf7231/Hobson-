## 1. Printed letter (on letterhead, hand-signed, real stamp)

[DATE]

Dr. [FIRST] [LAST]
[MAILING ADDRESS]

Dear Dr. [LAST],

I'm writing about your building at [PROPERTY ADDRESS].

Over a five-year period, I helped one investor client acquire more than $90 million of medical real estate, including ten sale-leasebacks with physicians who wanted to unlock the value of their building without leaving it. Along the way I returned more than $350,000 of my commission to that client.

Demand for well-located medical property in South Florida remains strong, and many owners who bought years ago are sitting on equity they could put to work. Depending on your plans, that can mean:

- **Selling outright**, quietly, to a qualified medical real estate buyer.
- **A sale-leaseback**: selling the building, staying on as the tenant under a long-term lease, and taking the equity out as cash. Nothing changes for your staff or patients.
- **Selling and exchanging** into a passive, single-tenant medical property through a 1031 exchange.

I'd be glad to prepare a confidential opinion of value for your building, showing an outright sale and a sale-leaseback side by side. There is no cost and no obligation, and nothing is ever listed or advertised without your say.

If you'd like one, simply text or email **BOV** with the property address, or call me directly at (561) 255-7285.

With respect,

## 3. Call opener (after skip trace)

"Dr. [LAST], this is Paul Schafranick with Next Endeavor CRE. I'll be brief. I have a buyer looking for medical buildings in [CITY], and your building at [ADDRESS] came up. Would you ever consider selling it, or doing a sale-leaseback where you stay as the tenant?"

- **If yes or maybe:** "Would a confidential valuation be helpful, showing both a sale and a sale-leaseback? I can have it to you this week."
- **If not a fit for your buyer but open to selling:** "Understood. I also represent owners directly and can market it quietly to other medical investors."
- **If no:** "Totally understand. Would it be alright if I checked in once a year?"
