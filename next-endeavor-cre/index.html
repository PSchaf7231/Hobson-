Not All NNN Properties Are Created Equal

Here's what separates a solid investment from a great one:

  • Investment-grade or corporate-guaranteed tenant
  • Long remaining lease term, ideally 10+ years
  • Scheduled rent increases built into the lease
  • Recession-resistant, essential business type
  • High daily traffic count: 3,000+ vehicles per day
  • Strong local population base: 10,000+ within 5 miles
  • Easy to re-tenant if the current occupant vacates
  • No landlord responsibilities: taxes, insurance and maintenance on the tenant
  • Medical tenants preferred: essential, insurance-backed, low turnover
NNN investing 101

What Is a Triple Net (NNN) Lease?

A triple net lease, or NNN lease, means the tenant is responsible for the three major property expenses: real estate taxes, building insurance, and maintenance costs. As the property owner, your job is simple: collect your check.

NReal estate taxes
NBuilding insurance
NMaintenance

NNN investments are favored by passive investors, retirees, 1031 exchange buyers, and anyone who wants the stability of real estate without the headaches of active management. When backed by a national or regional corporate tenant, a NNN property behaves more like a bond than a traditional real estate investment: predictable, stable, and built for the long term.

Why medical NNN specifically

Essential tenants. Steady checks.

  • Medical tenants are essential businesses. They keep operating through recessions.
  • Backed by insurance reimbursements, not consumer spending.
  • Lower turnover than retail. Doctors build their practices around their location.
  • Corporate or physician group guarantees provide an extra layer of security.
1031 exchanges

Selling an Investment Property? Let's Put That Equity to Work.

A 1031 exchange allows you to sell one investment property and reinvest the proceeds into a like-kind property, deferring your capital gains taxes. NNN properties are one of the most popular destinations for 1031 exchange buyers because of their stability, long lease terms, and passive income structure.

What is a NNN lease?

A triple net lease means the tenant pays property taxes, insurance, and maintenance, not you. As the owner, you collect rent and let the tenant handle the rest. It's one of the most hands-off real estate investments available.

Why focus on medical NNN properties?

Medical tenants are essential businesses. They keep operating through recessions, they're backed by insurance reimbursements rather than consumer spending, and they rarely relocate once established. That makes them among the most stable NNN tenants available.

Not at all. Next Endeavor CRE operates nationwide. Through our referral network, I can source and facilitate deals in any state. Most of my investor clients never set foot in the state where their property is located.

How does a 1031 Exchange work with NNN properties?

When you sell an investment property, a 1031 exchange lets you roll the proceeds into a like-kind property, deferring your capital gains tax. NNN properties are ideal exchange targets because of their long leases and passive income structure. Timing is critical, so reach out before your closing.

From there, I identify properties that match your criteria and walk you through the financials, lease terms, and due diligence process. You make the decisions. I make sure you have everything you need to make the right one.

Are your services free to me as a buyer?

In most cases, yes. Buyer's broker commissions are typically paid by the seller. And on qualifying deals, you actually walk away with a credit, not a cost.

Why are medical tenants considered safer than retail tenants?

Medical practices are essential services. People need healthcare regardless of the economy. Unlike retail, medical tenants aren't competing with online shopping or affected by consumer spending trends. They show up, they pay rent, and they stay.

What types of medical properties do you specialize in?

I focus on single-tenant medical buildings with NNN leases: specialty practices like spine care, dialysis centers, urgent care, physical therapy, and surgical centers. These tenants typically have long leases backed by physician groups or corporate operators.

What is a dual NNN lease in a medical building?

Some medical properties have two separate tenants, each with their own NNN lease, in one building. This structure reduces your risk, because if one tenant ever vacates, you still have income coming in from the other. It's a built-in hedge that most single-tenant properties can't offer.

Who guarantees the lease on a medical NNN property?

It depends on the tenant structure. The guarantee could come from an individual physician, a physician group, or a larger corporate operator. Corporate guarantees offer the strongest protection: the corporation itself, not just the property, is responsible for your monthly payment.

Are medical NNN properties good for 1031 exchanges?

They're one of the best options available. Long lease terms, stable essential-service tenants, and predictable income make medical NNN properties ideal replacement properties for 1031 exchange buyers who want to park equity somewhere safe, passive, and built to last.
