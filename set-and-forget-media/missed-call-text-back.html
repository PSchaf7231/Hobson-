Missed-call text-back for contractors

When you can't pick up, the caller gets a text from your business within seconds. The conversation keeps going, instead of the caller moving on to the next contractor on the list.

Call or text (561) 255-7285 Palm Beach County, Florida
Why it matters

You can't answer every call. You can answer every caller.

If you're on a roof, under a sink, or driving between jobs, the phone goes to voicemail. Plenty of people with an urgent problem never leave one. They hang up and call the next number in the search results.

Missed-call text-back sends them an immediate, friendly reply, so they know a real local business got their call and will get back to them. Many people would rather text anyway. They can describe the problem, send an address, and keep going with their day while you finish the job in front of you.

Who it's for

Solo operators and small crews where the owner is also the dispatcher, and trades that get urgent or after-hours calls. If you've ever called someone back an hour later and heard "we already found somebody," this is for you.

How it works

What happens when a call goes unanswered.

  1. 01

    Call is missed

    Your phone rings like normal. If nobody picks up, the system registers the missed call.

  2. 02

    Text goes out

    The caller gets a short text in your voice, like: "Hi, this is [your business]. Sorry we missed you, we're on a job. What can we help with?"

  3. 03

    Reply comes to you

    Their reply lands in one inbox on your phone. Answer between jobs, call back, or send a booking link.

  4. 04

    Nothing slips

    Every caller becomes a contact record, so you can see who reached out and who still needs an answer.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to change my phone number?

Usually not. Depending on your carrier and phone setup, unanswered calls are forwarded to the system, or we set up a new local number for your business. We go over the options with you before anything changes.

What happens if I answer the call?

Nothing. The text only goes out when a call is missed.

Can I keep certain numbers from getting a text?

Yes. Suppliers, family, or anyone else can go on a do-not-text list.

Is texting callers allowed?

Business texting has rules, so we set it up the way carriers require: a registered number, clear opt-out instructions, and STOP replies honored automatically. If you have specific legal questions about your marketing, check with your attorney.

Does it work nights and weekends?

Yes. It runs around the clock, and the after-hours message can say when you'll call back.
