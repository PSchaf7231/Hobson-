Review automation

Google review automation for contractors

After each finished job, your customer gets a short, friendly text asking for a Google review, with a link that opens the review form directly. No awkward asking in the driveway, and no remembering to send it three days later.

Why it matters

Most customers will leave a review. They just need to be asked.

Homeowners compare contractors on Google before they call. Recent reviews that mention the kind of work you do, and the towns you work in, help people pick you over someone they know nothing about.

The hard part isn't the customer. It's remembering to ask at the right moment, every time, while you're already thinking about the next job. Review automation makes the ask happen consistently, so the reviews keep coming in without you chasing them.

Playing by the rules

No fake reviews, no incentives, no gating.

Google's policies prohibit offering anything in exchange for a review, and they don't allow businesses to selectively ask only the customers they expect to be happy. Federal rules also prohibit fake or bought reviews. Breaking these rules can get reviews removed or your profile restricted.

So we set it up to ask every customer after every completed job, we never offer discounts or gifts for reviews, and we never write reviews on your behalf. If a customer replies to the request with a problem, that reply comes straight to you so you can make it right.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I choose who gets asked?

You choose which kinds of jobs trigger a request, for example completed jobs but not free estimates. Within that, we ask everyone, which keeps you in line with Google's guidelines.

What if someone leaves a bad review?

It happens to every business. You'll see it quickly and can reply calmly and specifically. Future customers read your replies too.

What about Yelp or Facebook?

The focus is Google, because that's where most local searches for contractors start. A Facebook link can be added. Yelp asks businesses not to solicit reviews, so we don't automate requests there.

Will this guarantee more reviews?

No one can promise what customers will do. What it does is make sure every customer gets asked, promptly, with a one-tap link, which is the step that usually gets skipped when you're busy.
