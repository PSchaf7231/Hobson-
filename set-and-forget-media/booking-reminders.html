Booking & reminders

Online booking and appointment reminders for contractors

Customers pick a time for an estimate or service call online, based on your real availability. Automatic text and email reminders go out before the visit, so fewer appointments get forgotten.

Why it matters

Less phone tag. Fewer wasted trips.

A homeowner who wants a quote at 9 p.m. shouldn't have to wait until you call back tomorrow, because by then they may have booked someone else. And a 30-minute drive to an estimate isn't worth much if nobody's home when you get there.

Online booking handles the first problem: people can commit the moment they're ready, day or night. Automatic reminders handle the second: customers get a heads-up before you arrive, with an easy way to reschedule instead of simply not showing up.

Who it's for

Contractors who run estimates, do recurring maintenance, or book service calls into time slots. It fits whether you want customers to book directly or just request a time for you to approve.

How it works

Set your rules once. The calendar does the rest.

  1. 01

    We set up your calendar

    Appointment types like free estimates or service calls, how long each takes, drive-time buffers, and the hours you take them.

  2. 02

    Customers book

    From your website, your Google Business Profile, or a link in a text. They only see open slots.

  3. 03

    Reminders go out

    An instant confirmation, then reminders before the visit, each with a link to reschedule. Timing is up to you.

  4. 04

    Everything syncs

    Bookings land on your calendar with the customer's name, address, and job details.

FAQ

Common questions

I don't want people booking any time they like. Can I limit it?

Yes. You set the days and hours, minimum notice, daily limits, and buffer time. You can also require your approval before any booking is confirmed.

Can I use arrival windows instead of exact times?

Yes. Slots can be set up as arrival windows, for example 8 to 10 a.m., instead of exact start times.

Will customers actually book online?

Some people will always prefer to call, and the phone still works. Online booking gives everyone else, especially people browsing after hours, a way to commit right away.

Does it work with my current calendar or software?

It syncs with Google Calendar and Outlook. If you use field-service software, tell us what it is and we'll tell you honestly whether it connects.
